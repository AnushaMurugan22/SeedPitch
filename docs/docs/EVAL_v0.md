# SeedPitch - Evaluation v0

## Objective
Evaluate the SeedPitch RAG question-answering system using 10 questions based on the ingested startup policy documents.

## Evaluation Criteria

Each question is scored using:
- Answer correctness: 0 or 1
- Citation correctness: 0 or 1

Maximum score: 20

## Evaluation Questions

| No. | Question | Expected Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the objective of the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme? | Support startups with proof of concept, prototype development, product trials, market entry and commercialization. |
| 2 | Which organization implements the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme? | Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT). |
| 3 | What is proof of concept? | Demonstration that an idea or technology is feasible. |
| 4 | What is prototype development? | Building an initial working model of a product. |
| 5 | What is the purpose of product trials? | Testing a product before commercial launch. |
| 6 | What is market entry support? | Helping startups introduce products or services into the market. |
| 7 | How can seed funding help startups? | It helps startups reach a stage where they can attract further investment or loans. |
| 8 | Who provides recommendations for selecting startups under the scheme? | The Incubator Seed Management Committee (ISMC). |
| 9 | What is the role of an incubator in the scheme? | Supporting and mentoring startups and administering seed funding. |
| 10 | Can startups use the scheme for commercialization? | Yes, the scheme supports commercialization of products and services. |

## Scoring

- Answer correctness: 1 point if the answer matches the source document.
- Citation correctness: 1 point if the cited document and page support the answer.
- Maximum score: 20 points.
- Final accuracy = (Total score / 20) * 100.

## Notes

Evaluation scores must be filled after testing each question against the actual source documents. Do not mark untested answers as correct.
